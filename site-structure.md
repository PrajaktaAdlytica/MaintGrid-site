Use this file together with `brief.md` and `AGENTS.md` when building.

### Section 12: FAQ

Badge:

- FAQ

H2:

- Questions maintenance leaders ask before switching.

Questions:

1. How is MaintGrid different from a normal CMMS?
   - MaintGrid is built around connected maintenance data. Work orders, parts, failures, risk, and asset history are linked so teams can understand what happened, what is needed, and what risk comes next.
2. Can MaintGrid replace spreadsheets?
   - Yes. Teams can start by moving work orders, assets, spare parts, and recurring issue logs into MaintGrid, then expand into risk tracking and integrations.
3. Do we need sensors or IoT data to use MaintGrid Risk?
   - No. MaintGrid Risk can start with work history, failure records, asset criticality, part availability, and downtime impact. Sensor data can be added later where available.
4. Can MaintGrid connect with ERP or WMS tools?
   - The site should say MaintGrid is designed for integration planning with ERP, WMS, and existing operational systems. Avoid promising specific integrations until implemented.
5. Does MaintGrid work for multiple sites?
   - Yes. The Operations and Enterprise plans should support multi-site visibility, site-level dashboards, and shared asset and parts reporting.
6. Is MaintGrid suitable for EU companies?
   - Yes. MaintGrid is positioned as an EU-based platform with data, access, and operational workflows designed for European industrial teams.
